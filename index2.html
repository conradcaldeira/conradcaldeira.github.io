About Conrad

A Mumbai soul
navigating the world.

I grew up watching cargo ships pass Mumbai's horizon, never imagining I'd one day be the person moving what was on them. Over 10 years in logistics and B2B sales across the GCC and India, I've learned that the cargo is never just cargo — behind every shipment is a person, a story, a risk someone took.

I chose sales because I love people. I stayed in logistics because it keeps sending me to new ports, new conversations, new worlds. Faith has been my compass through all of it.

Now I'm learning Portuguese — the language of the explorers who first sailed to my city five centuries ago. Every port has a story. I'm still writing mine.
