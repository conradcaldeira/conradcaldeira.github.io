A builder at heart.
A salesman by profession.

I'm Conrad — a logistics and sales professional based in Kuwait, currently with world’s leading logistics ecosystems, one of the world's leading enablers of global trade. My work sits at the intersection of commerce, relationships, and complex supply chains across the GCC.
